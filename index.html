(SLOW ARCHIVE)

Slow Technology challenges the idea that technology must always prioritize speed, efficiency, and convenience. Instead, it encourages a more intentional relationship with digital experiences, valuing reflection, attention, and meaningful human connections. From digital minimalism to alternative web design, Slow Technology explores how we can rethink the role of technology in our lives—not by rejecting it, but by using it more thoughtfully.
